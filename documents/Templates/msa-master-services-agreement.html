ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party.
1.2 "Confidential Information" has the meaning set forth in Article 6.
1.3 "Deliverables" means the tangible outputs, reports, software, documentation, or other work product specified in a Statement of Work to be delivered by Contractor to Client.
1.4 "Effective Date" means the date first written above when this Agreement is executed by both Parties.
1.5 "Intellectual Property Rights" means patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, moral rights, and other intellectual property rights worldwide.
1.6 "Services" means the professional services to be performed by Contractor as described in a Statement of Work.
1.7 "Statement of Work" or "SOW" means a written document executed by both Parties that describes specific Services, Deliverables, fees, and timelines for a particular engagement.
1.8 "Term" has the meaning set forth in Article 10.

ARTICLE 2 — SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 In accordance with this Agreement, Client hereby engages Contractor, and Contractor accepts such engagement, to perform the Services and provide the Deliverables as described in one or more Statements of Work.
2.2 Each SOW shall specify, at a minimum: (a) description of Services and Deliverables; (b) applicable timelines and milestones; (c) fees and payment terms; (d) personnel assigned; and (e) any special terms or conditions.
2.3 In the event of any conflict between the terms of this Agreement and an SOW, the terms of this Agreement shall prevail, except that the specific terms of an SOW shall control with respect to the particular engagement covered by that SOW.
2.4 Contractor shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards for similar services.

ARTICLE 3 — FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 Fees

Client shall pay Contractor the fees set forth in each SOW. All fees are in United States Dollars unless otherwise stated.

3.2 Invoicing

Contractor shall submit invoices in accordance with the payment schedule specified in each SOW. Unless otherwise stated in an SOW, invoices are due within fifteen (15) days of receipt.

3.3 Late Payments

Overdue amounts shall bear interest at the rate of 1.5% per month (18% APR) or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less. Client shall also reimburse Contractor for all reasonable costs of collection, including attorney's fees.

3.4 Expenses

Unless otherwise stated in an SOW, Contractor's fees are all-inclusive and Client shall not be responsible for any out-of-pocket expenses. Travel expenses must be pre-approved in writing by Client and will be billed at cost.

3.5 Taxes

Fees do not include any sales, use, or value-added taxes. Client is responsible for all such taxes except for taxes on Contractor's net income.

ARTICLE 4 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS

4.1 Client Materials

Client retains all ownership rights to its pre-existing materials, data, and Intellectual Property Rights provided to Contractor for purposes of performing the Services ("Client Materials"). Client grants Contractor a limited, non-exclusive license to use Client Materials solely for the purpose of providing the Services under this Agreement.

4.2 Contractor Background IP

Contractor retains all ownership rights to its pre-existing intellectual property, methodologies, frameworks, tools, and know-how used in performing the Services ("Contractor Background IP"). Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of Contractor Background IP to Client.

4.3 Work Product

Upon full payment of all fees, all Deliverables created specifically for Client under an SOW shall become the exclusive property of Client. Contractor hereby assigns to Client all right, title, and interest in and to such Deliverables, excluding Contractor Background IP incorporated therein.

4.4 License to Background IP

To the extent any Contractor Background IP is incorporated into Deliverables, Contractor grants Client a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use such Background IP as part of or in connection with the Deliverables.

4.5 Moral Rights

To the extent permitted by law, Contractor waives any moral rights in the Deliverables assigned to Client under this Agreement.

ARTICLE 5 — CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Mutual Obligations

Each Party ("Disclosing Party") may disclose Confidential Information to the other Party ("Receiving Party"). The Receiving Party agrees to:

  • (a) hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence;
  • (b) not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent;
  • (c) use Confidential Information solely for purposes of this Agreement;
  • (d) limit access to employees and contractors who have a need to know and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as this Article.
5.2 Definition

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by the Disclosing Party, whether orally or in writing, that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information and circumstances of disclosure. This includes business plans, technical data, customer information, financial data, and project details.

5.3 Exceptions

Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) was publicly known prior to disclosure; (b) becomes publicly known through no wrongful act of the Receiving Party; (c) was rightfully received from a third party without breach; (d) was independently developed without use of the Confidential Information; or (e) must be disclosed by law or court order (with prompt notice to the Disclosing Party).

5.4 Return or Destruction

Upon termination of this Agreement or at the Disclosing Party's request, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all copies of Confidential Information and certify such destruction in writing.

5.5 Survival

The obligations of this Article shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years, except for trade secrets which shall remain protected indefinitely.

ARTICLE 6 — DATA PROTECTION AND SECURITY

6.1 Data Protection Standards

Contractor shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Client data processed under this Agreement, including encryption, access controls, and security monitoring.

6.2 Breach Notification

Contractor shall notify Client within forty-eight (48) hours of becoming aware of any actual or suspected unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss of Client data.

6.3 Compliance

Contractor represents that it complies with applicable data protection laws and regulations, including but not limited to NIST standards, CMMC requirements, and relevant federal cybersecurity mandates where applicable.

ARTICLE 7 — WARRANTIES AND DISCLAIMERS

7.1 Contractor Warranty

Contractor warrants that: (a) Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner; (b) Deliverables will conform to specifications in the applicable SOW; and (c) Contractor has the right to enter into this Agreement and perform the Services.

7.2 Remedy for Non-Conformance

Client's sole remedy for breach of the warranty in Section 7.1(b) is that Contractor shall, at no additional cost, correct or replace non-conforming Deliverables within a reasonable time after notice from Client.

7.3 Disclaimer

EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS ARTICLE, CONTRACTOR MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE, OR NON-INFRINGEMENT. CONTRACTOR DOES NOT WARRANT THAT THE SERVICES WILL BE UNINTERRUPTED OR ERROR-FREE.

ARTICLE 8 — LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

8.1 Cap on Liability

IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID BY CLIENT TO CONTRACTOR UNDER THE SOW GIVING RISE TO SUCH CLAIM IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

8.2 Consequential Damages Waiver

IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOST PROFITS, LOST REVENUE, LOSS OF GOODWILL, OR DATA LOSS, WHETHER BASED IN CONTRACT, TORT, OR OTHERWISE, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

8.3 Exceptions

The limitations in Sections 8.1 and 8.2 shall not apply to: (a) either Party's obligation to pay fees; (b) either Party's breach of confidentiality obligations; (c) gross negligence or willful misconduct; or (d) claims arising from infringement of Intellectual Property Rights.

ARTICLE 9 — INDEMNIFICATION

9.1 Contractor Indemnification

Contractor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Client and its Affiliates from and against any third-party claims, losses, damages, liabilities, and expenses (including reasonable attorney's fees) arising out of: (a) Contractor's gross negligence or willful misconduct; or (b) allegations that the Deliverables infringe a third party's Intellectual Property Rights.

9.2 Client Indemnification

Client shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Contractor and its Affiliates from and against any third-party claims arising out of: (a) Client's use of Deliverables in a manner not authorized by this Agreement; or (b) Client's negligence or willful misconduct.

9.3 Procedure

The indemnified Party shall promptly notify the indemnifying Party of any claim, cooperate in the defense, and allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement of the claim.

ARTICLE 10 — TERM AND TERMINATION

10.1 Term

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of [TWO / THREE / FIVE] years ("Initial Term"), unless earlier terminated as provided herein. This Agreement may be renewed for successive one-year periods upon mutual written agreement.

10.2 Termination for Convenience

Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any outstanding SOW for convenience upon thirty (30) days written notice to the other Party.

10.3 Termination for Cause

Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within ten (10) days of receiving written notice of the breach.

10.4 Effect of Termination

Upon termination: (a) Client shall pay Contractor for all Services performed and expenses incurred through the termination date; (b) Contractor shall deliver all completed Deliverables; (c) each Party shall return or destroy the other's Confidential Information; and (d) all outstanding SOWs shall be cancelled except as expressly agreed otherwise.

10.5 Survival

The following Articles shall survive termination: 4 (Intellectual Property), 5 (Confidentiality), 7.3 (Disclaimer), 8 (Limitation of Liability), 9 (Indemnification), 12 (Governing Law), and 13 (Miscellaneous).

ARTICLE 11 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

11.1 Independent Contractor

Contractor is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, or joint venture relationship. Contractor has no authority to bind Client to any agreement.

11.2 Assignment

Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that either Party may assign this Agreement to an Affiliate or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets.

11.3 Force Majeure

Neither Party shall be liable for any delay or failure to perform due to causes beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, natural disasters, war, terrorism, government actions, epidemics, or interruptions of telecommunications or power infrastructure.

11.4 Notices

All notices shall be in writing and delivered by email with confirmation of receipt, certified mail, or recognized overnight courier to the addresses set forth in the preamble.

11.5 Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with all SOWs and exhibits, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written.

11.6 Amendments

This Agreement may only be amended or modified by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

11.7 Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect. The Parties shall negotiate in good faith to replace the invalid provision with a valid one that achieves the original intent.

11.8 Waiver

No waiver of any term of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing. A waiver shall not constitute a continuing waiver unless expressly stated.
