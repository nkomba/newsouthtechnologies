Form-Specific Privacy Notice

Last updated: July 22, 2026

NewSouth Technologies collects personal information through our website forms for specific business purposes. This notice explains exactly what we collect, where it goes, how it's protected, and how long we keep it.

2. Where Your Data Goes (Form Processors)

Our forms follow two different paths, and it matters which one your submission takes:

  • Contact, Audit/Consultation, CMMC, Training, and Session-Feedback forms are handled by Formspree, a third-party form provider. Your submission is transmitted to Formspree and delivered to us by email. While in transit and briefly at rest on Formspree's systems, that provider's security controls apply in addition to ours.
  • The Careers / Job Application form is handled by our own first-party serverless endpoint and stored in US government cloud. It is not routed through a commercial form provider.
Form(s) Processor Data Collected Location & Certification
Contact, Audit/Consultation, CMMC, Training, Feedback Formspree (third-party form provider) Name, work email, organization, message/inquiry details US-based AWS infrastructure · SOC 2 Type II · GDPR-compliant
Careers / Job Application New South Technologies serverless endpoint (first-party) Resume/CV, security clearance status, contact details, EEO data US-based Azure Government Cloud · FISMA Moderate · NIST SP 800-171 (resumes encrypted at rest with AES-256 — see Section 3)

Important: We do NOT sell, trade, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. Your information is only used for the purpose you submitted it. For the Formspree-handled forms above, your data also passes through that provider's infrastructure; we have configured it with encryption and access controls, and it processes data only on our behalf.

3. Encryption and Security Measures

In Transit

  • All form submissions are transmitted over TLS 1.3 (HTTPS)
  • Cloudflare SSL certificates verify server identity
  • HSTS (HTTP Strict Transport Security) enforced site-wide

At Rest

  • Resumes and attachments are encrypted using AES-256 (FIPS 197 validated)
  • Database records are encrypted using AES-256-GCM
  • Encryption keys managed via AWS KMS / Azure Key Vault

Access Controls

  • Mandatory Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for all staff accounts
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) — only HR and hiring managers can view resumes
  • Access logging enabled; audit logs retained for 1 year
  • Quarterly access reviews performed

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Data Type Retention Period Deletion Method
Careers resumes (rejected applicants) 18 months from submission Secure deletion per NIST 800-88 Rev. 1
Careers resumes (accepted candidates) 7 years from hire date Archived in HRIS; secure deletion after period
Consultation inquiries (no engagement) 1 year from last contact Secure deletion
Active client records 7 years post-engagement NIST 800-88 compliant destruction

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request erasure of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Portability: Receive your data in machine-readable format
  • Withdraw Consent: Opt out of future communications

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@newsouthtechnologies.com.

6. Breach Notification

If we discover unauthorized access to your personal data, we will:

  1. Notify affected individuals within 72 hours of discovery
  2. Provide details about what data was involved and steps you can take
  3. Report to applicable regulatory authorities as required by law

See our full Privacy Policy for additional details.
