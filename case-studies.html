Legacy Modernization · Cabinet-level federal agency

Retiring a legacy mainframe one auditable slice at a time

Challenge

An aging mainframe carries mission-critical business logic, compliance tracking is manual, and interdependencies are poorly documented — making every change slow and every audit painful.

Approach

We decouple business logic in incremental strangler-fig slices, landing each in a FedRAMP-aligned environment with identity, logging, encryption, and network controls documented from day one — no big-bang cutover, no planned mission downtime.

Evidence & Outcome

Each increment ships with a control-to-requirement traceability matrix and a compliance evidence pack mapped to NIST SP 800-53 and FedRAMP baselines — the artifacts an Authorizing Official or Inspector General actually asks for.

Governed Agility · State government agency

Moving toward continuous authorization without slowing releases

Challenge

Delivery teams want to ship faster, but each release triggers an authorization scramble, and controls are verified once a year rather than continuously.

Approach

We embed controls as code in the delivery pipeline so compliance evidence is generated as a byproduct of every build, moving the program toward continuous authorization (cATO) in partnership with the authorizing official.

Evidence & Outcome

An always-current control inventory, automated test and scan results tied to each release, and an immutable change history — so authorization can be maintained continuously instead of reconstructed at audit time.

Responsible AI · Federal program office

Standing up an AI-assisted workflow designed to pass review

Challenge

A program wants to adopt an AI- or LLM-assisted workflow but must satisfy NIST AI RMF expectations and OMB M-26-04 procurement requirements before it can be authorized.

Approach

We put data governance, bias testing, explainability, and continuous monitoring in place and map each to the applicable framework, so the system is designed to pass review rather than retrofitted to it.

Evidence & Outcome

A documented data-governance and bias-testing record and an explainability matrix, packaged so an authorizing official can trace how the system behaves and why.
