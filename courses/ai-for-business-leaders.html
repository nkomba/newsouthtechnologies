AI for Business Leaders

A five-day, non-technical executive program that takes business leaders from AI fundamentals to a governed 90-day action plan.

Course overview

AI for Business Leaders is a five-day, instructor-led executive program that takes leaders from AI fundamentals to a concrete, governed 90-day action plan for their business unit. It is deliberately non-technical — strategic, practical, and decision-oriented — and mirrors the New South Technologies philosophy: governance-first, responsible AI aligned to the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, with “proof an inspector can follow.”

Who it’s for: Executives, directors, senior managers, and business-unit, transformation, and program/portfolio leaders. No technical background required — participants bring a real business problem that becomes their capstone. Format: 5-day cohort, delivered virtually or on-site in the D.C. metro.

What you’ll be able to do

  • Explain AI, machine learning, and generative AI in plain language
  • Identify and prioritize AI opportunities using value, feasibility, and risk
  • Make build / buy / partner and operating-model decisions
  • Apply responsible-AI principles and the NIST AI RMF (Govern, Map, Measure, Manage)
  • Lead AI adoption and organizational change
  • Produce a realistic, governed 90-day AI action plan

Curriculum

  1. Day 1 — AI Foundations for Business Leaders. AI, ML, and generative AI in plain language; how models learn; capabilities vs. limits; common misconceptions; and data readiness. Anchor exercise: Build Your Shared AI Language.
  2. Day 2 — Business Value & Use-Case Identification. The four value levers — Grow, Save, Protect, Delight — and framing every use case to a measurable metric and baseline. Anchor exercise: complete an AI Opportunity Canvas.
  3. Day 3 — AI Strategy & Operating Model. Build / buy / partner sourcing and operating-model design — buy the commodity, build the differentiator, partner to learn. Anchor exercise: Sourcing & Operating-Model Design.
  4. Day 4 — Responsible AI, Governance, Ethics & Risk. Responsible-AI principles and the NIST AI RMF; matching human oversight to the stakes of each decision. Anchor exercise: run an AI Risk Assessment.
  5. Day 5 — Adoption, Change Leadership & Action Planning. Leading adoption and change, then assembling the week’s artifacts into a governed 90-day plan. Anchor exercise: Capstone Build + Presentations.

Frameworks you’ll leave using

  • Four value levers — Grow, Save, Protect, Delight — so every use case ladders to a measurable outcome
  • Value × Feasibility × Risk for judging any AI opportunity on more than one axis
  • Buy / Build / Partner sourcing decisions
  • The oversight dial — human-in-the-loop, on-the-loop, out-of-the-loop — matched to stakes
  • NIST AI RMF — Govern, Map, Measure, Manage — as the governance backbone
