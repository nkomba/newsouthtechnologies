New South Technologies Training

Strangler-Fig Migration for Federal IT Teams

Learn to modernize a legacy mainframe incrementally — no big-bang cutover, no mission downtime.

Course overview

A practitioner course on the strangler-fig pattern for public-sector modernization. Teams learn to identify seams in a legacy system, route traffic through a facade, migrate functionality slice by slice into governed cloud services, and retire the mainframe safely — keeping the mission running and auditors satisfied throughout.

Who it’s for: Federal/state application architects, delivery leads, and modernization program managers. Format: 3-day cohort, delivered virtually or on-site in the D.C. metro.

What you’ll be able to do

  • Map a legacy system and identify safe migration seams
  • Stand up a routing facade to move traffic incrementally
  • Migrate and verify functionality slice by slice
  • Plan a safe mainframe retirement with rollback options

Curriculum

  1. 1. Legacy assessment & seam identification
  2. 2. The facade pattern & incremental routing
  3. 3. Slice migration, data strategy & verification
  4. 4. Cutover, retirement & compliance evidence
