Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2, 2026

New South Technologies, LLC ("New South Technologies," "we," "our," or "us") is a Washington, D.C. metropolitan-area (DC–MD–VA) firm that modernizes legacy systems for federal civilian agencies and state governments. We take the same governance-first approach to your data that we bring to our engagements: we collect only what we need, we tell you exactly where it goes, and we keep it only as long as we should.

This Privacy Policy explains what we collect through newsouthtechnologies.com (the "Site"), why, and the choices you have. For the specific mechanics of each web form — the processor, data flow, encryption, and retention — see our companion Form Submissions Privacy Notice, which this policy incorporates by reference.

In plain terms: we are a business-to-government firm. We do not run an online store, we do not sell your data, and we do not use advertising trackers. Most people who reach us simply fill out a contact form.

1. Scope

This policy applies to information we collect from visitors to the Site and from people who contact us or apply for jobs through the Site. It does not apply to data we process on behalf of a client under a services contract — that data is governed by the applicable contract, statement of work, and federal or state law.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the categories of information below.

2.1 Information you provide through our forms

When you submit a consultation or audit request, training inquiry, CMMC self-assessment, general contact message, or job application, we collect the details you enter. Depending on the form, that may include your name, work email, phone number, organization or agency, your message or inquiry, and — for job applications only — your résumé/CV, professional certifications, work-authorization status, and security-clearance status. The exact fields per form are itemized in the Form Submissions Privacy Notice.

2.2 Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our infrastructure and security provider (Cloudflare) automatically logs limited technical data needed to deliver the Site securely — such as IP address, browser and device type, pages requested, and timestamps. We use this to serve pages, maintain security, and diagnose problems.

2.3 What we do not collect

We do not collect payment-card or financial-account details, government-issued identifiers (such as SSNs), or special categories of personal data through the Site, and we ask that you not submit classified information or controlled unclassified information (CUI) through public forms.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

  • respond to your inquiry and, where relevant, prepare a proposal or route your message to the right specialist;
  • evaluate and communicate with job applicants;
  • operate, secure, maintain, and improve the Site;
  • meet legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations, including recordkeeping applicable to federal contractors; and
  • send you information you have requested or, where permitted, occasional updates you can opt out of at any time.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Where a legal basis is required (for example, under the GDPR for visitors in applicable jurisdictions), we rely on: our legitimate interests in responding to inquiries and operating our business; steps taken at your request before entering an agreement (such as processing a job application or a consultation request); compliance with a legal obligation; and, where applicable, your consent (which you may withdraw at any time).

5. How Your Information Is Processed and Shared

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. We share information only as follows:

  • Form processors. General website forms (contact, audit/consultation, CMMC, training, and feedback) are processed by Formspree, a U.S.-based third-party form provider (SOC 2 Type II) that transmits your submission to us by email and processes it only on our behalf. The careers/job-application form is handled by our own first-party serverless endpoint hosted in U.S. Azure Government Cloud (FISMA Moderate; aligned to NIST SP 800-171), not through a commercial form provider. Details are in the Form Submissions Privacy Notice.
  • Infrastructure providers. Providers such as Cloudflare deliver and protect the Site under contractual confidentiality and security obligations.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information where required by law, subpoena, or lawful government request, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of New South Technologies, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers. If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

All service providers are bound to use your data only for the purposes we specify and in line with applicable law.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We manage cookies through a consent management platform (Cookiebot), which categorizes cookies and records your choices. Cookies fall into standard categories: strictly necessary cookies (required to deliver and secure the Site, including Cloudflare security cookies) are always active, while preference, statistics, and marketing cookies load only if and when you accept them. When you first visit the Site, the cookie banner lets you accept, reject, or choose categories, and you can change or withdraw your consent at any time using the banner's controls. We do not sell your information, and we do not use cookies to do so. You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings; disabling strictly necessary cookies may affect how the Site functions.

7. Data Security

We apply the safeguards described in our Form Submissions Privacy Notice, including:

  • In transit: all submissions are transmitted over TLS 1.3 (HTTPS), with HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS) enforced and TLS certificates validating server identity.
  • At rest: résumés and attachments are encrypted with AES-256 (FIPS 197 validated); database records are encrypted with AES-256-GCM; keys are managed via AWS KMS / Azure Key Vault.
  • Access: multi-factor authentication for staff accounts, role-based access limiting who can view submissions, access logging, and periodic access reviews.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information using measures appropriate to its sensitivity.

8. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected or as required by law. Our standard periods (detailed in the Form Submissions Privacy Notice) include:

  • Consultation/inquiry submissions (no engagement): up to 1 year from last contact, then securely deleted.
  • Job-application résumés (not hired): 18 months from submission, then securely deleted per NIST SP 800-88.
  • Job-application résumés (hired): 7 years from hire date, archived in our HRIS.
  • Active client records: 7 years post-engagement, then destroyed per NIST SP 800-88.
  • Automatic/technical logs: retained for a limited period for security and diagnostics.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate data, request deletion (subject to legal and contractual retention requirements), receive your data in a portable format, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may also opt out of any non-essential communications at any time.

To exercise a right, contact our Privacy Officer using the details in Section 12. We will verify your request and respond within the timeframe required by applicable law (generally within 30 days). Exercising these rights will not result in discriminatory treatment.

10. Breach Notification

If we become aware of unauthorized access to your personal data, we will notify affected individuals and applicable regulatory authorities as required by law, describe the data involved, and outline steps you can take. Where practicable, we aim to notify affected individuals within 72 hours of confirming a reportable incident.

11. Children's Privacy

The Site and our services are directed to businesses and government organizations, not to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

12. Contact Us

Questions, concerns, or requests about this policy or your data can be directed to our Privacy Officer:

  • Privacy Officer: New South Technologies, LLC
  • Email: privacy@newsouthtechnologies.com
  • Location: Washington, D.C. Metro (DC–MD–VA)

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our practices or legal obligations change. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date above, post the current version here, and — for material changes — provide a more prominent notice on the Site. Your continued use of the Site after an update takes effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
