Why "governed agility" beats pure speed

In federal IT, velocity without governance is a liability: every release that outruns its authorization is an audit finding waiting to happen. But heavyweight, document-driven governance is just as costly — it turns a six-week feature into a six-month approval cycle. Governed Agility resolves the tension by making compliance an automated property of the pipeline rather than a manual gate at the end of it.

The result is delivery that is both fast and defensible: controls are enforced as code, evidence is generated automatically, and authorization becomes continuous instead of a periodic scramble.

Our approach

  • Controls as code. Security and compliance requirements are expressed as policy-as-code and enforced automatically in the pipeline, so non-compliant changes never reach production.
  • Automated evidence. Every build, test, scan, and deployment emits traceable artifacts, giving you an always-current control inventory instead of a stale spreadsheet.
  • Shift-left security. Static analysis, dependency scanning, and infrastructure checks run on every commit — vulnerabilities are caught while they are cheap to fix.
  • Continuous authorization (cATO). Ongoing monitoring and automated evidence let you maintain authorization continuously rather than re-earning it every three years.
  • Zero unplanned downtime. Progressive delivery, automated rollback, and observability keep releases safe and reversible.

What you get

  • A DevSecOps pipeline with compliance gates enforced automatically
  • An always-current, audit-ready control and evidence inventory
  • A path to continuous authorization (cATO) with your authorizing official

Frequently asked questions

Does adding governance slow the team down?

Done right, it speeds them up. Because checks are automated and run continuously, teams get instant feedback instead of waiting on a late-stage review board — and they stop re-doing work that fails compliance at the end.

What is continuous authorization (cATO)?

Rather than a point-in-time authorization that expires, cATO uses ongoing monitoring and automated evidence to maintain an authorization continuously, provided the controls keep passing. We help you build the monitoring and evidence pipeline your authorizing official needs to grant it.

Can you work with our existing toolchain?

Yes. We integrate governance into the CI/CD and cloud platforms you already use rather than forcing a rip-and-replace of your tooling.
