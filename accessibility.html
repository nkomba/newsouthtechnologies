Accessibility Statement

New South Technologies is committed to digital accessibility for people of all abilities.

Our Commitment

New South Technologies is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards.

Conformance Status

This website is designed to conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA and Section 508 requirements. Our most recent evaluation was conducted on July 22, 2026 using a combination of automated tooling and manual assistive-technology testing.

  • Perceivable: All content is presented in ways users can perceive.
  • Operable: Navigation and controls work with keyboard and assistive technologies.
  • Understandable: Content and UI operate in predictable ways.
  • Robust: Content works across current and future user agents, including assistive technologies.

Known Limitations

While we strive for full compliance, some areas may still present challenges. We are currently conducting a comprehensive audit and will update this section with findings as they are remediated.

Technical Information

This website uses the following technologies to support accessibility:

  • Semantic HTML structure
  • ARIA landmarks and roles
  • Skip navigation links
  • Visible focus indicators

Interactive Components

Our before/after comparison displays are fully keyboard-accessible and screen-reader compatible. Users can toggle between states using button controls that work with keyboard (Tab, Enter, Space) and are announced by assistive technologies.

Feedback and Support

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our website:

  • Email: accessibility@newsouthtechnologies.com
  • Response timeframe: We aim to respond within 5 business days.
